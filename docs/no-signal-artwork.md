# No-signal artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Asset: `uap-lab/static/ufo-no-signal.png`.

Prompt: 16:9 vintage 1950s monochrome television calibration test card inspired by the classic Indian-head test pattern, replacing the portrait with a friendly grey alien head and tiny flying saucer. Symmetrical concentric circles, radial resolution wedges, black-and-white bars, crosshairs, corner targets; ivory and charcoal with subtle analog texture. Exact text: “NO SIGNAL” and “PLEASE STAND BY”. Full canvas, no bezel or perspective.

The UI displays this artwork when preview frames stop advancing or the known Cam Link no-signal slate is recognized. Slate matching requires both the logo and lettering; dark sky alone is not a match. The detector runs at most once per second on preview images. Raw capture and evidence images are never replaced with artwork.
